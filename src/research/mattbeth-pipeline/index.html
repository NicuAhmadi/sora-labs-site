Why this matters for lead selection

For Matthias, the best-fit opportunities are not generic new construction. They are leased spaces, HQ relocations, retail fit-outs, and interior projects where a tenant or owner needs a reliable local GC who can move fast, coordinate trades cleanly, and protect opening timelines. That is exactly the lane these leads were filtered for.

  • Tri-county coverage
  • Retail and commercial interior experience
  • Open-store / occupied-space sensitivity
  • Brand-driven finish standards
  • Local owner access instead of bureaucratic handoffs

Method and market notes

Every lead in this briefing was pulled from live commercial real estate sources dated within the last 90 days, then cross-checked to confirm the project hasn't already been completed. If a lead couldn't be verified with a dated source and a current-reality check, it didn't make the cut. No padding, no guesswork — just opportunities that are actually in motion right now.

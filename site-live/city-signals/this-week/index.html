04Method

How these are calculated

  • This week is the latest 7 days with published data. DataSF publishes about a day behind, so the week ends on the newest case date in the data, not today.
  • Spikes compare reports in the last 7 days with the daily average over the 30 days before that, scaled to 7 days. A pair is flagged at 25% or more above that average with at least 20 reports. If none qualifies, the finding shows the largest drop instead.
  • Repeat street and request types group reports by street name and request subtype over 90 days; a long street counts as one pair. If none qualifies, the finding shows how many older cases are still open.
  • Time to close compares the median days from request to closure in the city's system for the 5 highest-volume categories: cases closed in the last 30 days against cases closed 31 to 60 days ago. A category is flagged when its median rose by 3 days or more from at least 3. If none qualifies, the finding shows the category with the shortest median time to close.
  • Each record is a service request, not a verified incident, and a closed case may be administratively closed rather than fixed. The sentences on this page are fixed templates filled with computed numbers; no AI writes them.

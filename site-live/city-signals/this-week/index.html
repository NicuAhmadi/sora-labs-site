How these are calculated

  • This week is the latest 7 days with published data. DataSF publishes about a day behind, so the week ends on the newest case date in the data, not today.
  • Spikes compare reports in the last 7 days with the daily average over the 30 days before that, scaled to 7 days. A pair is flagged at 25% or more above that average with at least 20 reports. If none qualifies, the card shows the largest drop instead.
  • Repeat locations group reports by street and request subtype over 90 days. If none qualifies, the card shows how many cases older than 14 days are still open.
  • Closure times compare the median days to close for the 5 highest-volume categories: cases closed in the last 30 days against cases closed 31 to 60 days ago. A category is flagged when its median rose by 3 days or more from at least 3. If none qualifies, the card shows the fastest resolving category.
  • Each record is a service request, not a verified incident, and a closed case may be administratively closed rather than fixed. The sentences on this page are fixed templates filled with computed numbers; no AI writes them.
